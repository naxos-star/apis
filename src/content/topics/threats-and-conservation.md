---
title: Threats and how to help
summary: Varroa mites, pesticides, disease and habitat loss all put pressure on honey bees.
order: 4
---
Managed honey bee colonies face several overlapping pressures.

- **Varroa destructor** is a parasitic mite that feeds on bees and spreads viruses. It is widely regarded as the biggest threat to managed colonies.
- **Pesticides**, especially some insecticides, can harm foraging and navigation even at low doses.
- **Habitat loss** reduces the variety of flowers available through the season.
- **Disease and weather stress** add to the strain, particularly over winter.

You can help by planting a succession of flowers that bloom from early spring to autumn, avoiding pesticide use on flowering plants, leaving some wild areas, and buying honey from local beekeepers. Honey bees are one of many pollinators, so supporting wild bees helps too.
